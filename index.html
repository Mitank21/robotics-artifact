What this is

A directory of robotics and automation companies, taken from the Google Sheet “Robotics & Automation Directory – MASTER”. It has two lists: India (companies based in India or with an India presence) and World (companies elsewhere). The two lists do not overlap and are never mixed.

Columns

  • Company, Website, LinkedIn: as listed in the sheet. Click a row to see the links.
  • Category: the sector the sheet puts the company in. The same colour is used for a category everywhere on this page.
  • HQ city: headquarters city. Some companies in the India list have headquarters abroad.
  • India presence (India list only): the sheet’s free-text note. The filter groups it into “HQ in India” and “Office or R&D in India” (everything that is not exactly “HQ”).
  • Country (World list only): country of headquarters.
  • Headcount: the size band as listed in the sheet. “Not listed” means the sheet has no value.
  • What they do: a one-line description, shown when you open a row.

Where the data comes from

This page shows a saved copy of the sheet, taken on 19 November 2026. Changes made to the sheet afterwards do not appear here until a new copy is attached.
